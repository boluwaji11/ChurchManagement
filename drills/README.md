# Drill records

R21.6. The census files from each quarterly restore drill, and nothing else.

A census is a count of every row of every table for one church, taken before a restore and compared
against the restored copy. Keeping them here is what makes `docs/backup-and-restore.md` a record
rather than a plan.

The files hold counts and digests. They hold no names, no addresses and no church data, which is
why they are committed: a drill record kept on somebody's laptop is a drill nobody can check.
